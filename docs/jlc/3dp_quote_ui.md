# 3D Technology
  ## Descriptions
  - **SLA (Stereolithography):** Uses UV lasers to cure liquid resin layer by layer, delivering high accuracy, smooth surface finish, and fine details. Suited to prototypes, models, and complex designs. [Learn More](https://jlc3dp.com/help/article/what-is-sla-3d-printing)
  - **MJF (Multi Jet Fusion):** Fuses nylon powder using agent deposition and heat, producing strong, isotropic parts without support structures. Suited to functional prototypes and end-use components. [Learn More](https://jlc3dp.com/help/article/What-is-Multi-Jet-Fusion(MJF)3D-Printing)
  - **SLM (Selective Laser Melting):** Melts metal powder with a high-energy laser to create dense metal parts with excellent strength and heat resistance. Commonly used in aerospace and industrial applications. [Learn More](https://jlc3dp.com/help/article/What-is-SLM-3D-Printing)
  - **FDM (Fused Deposition Modeling):** Builds parts by extruding thermoplastic filament layer by layer. A cost-effective option for prototypes, education, and low-volume production. [Learn More](https://jlc3dp.com/help/article/What-is-FDM-3D-Printing)
  - **SLS (Selective Laser Sintering):** Sinters nylon powder layer by layer to create durable parts without support structures. Suited to complex geometries, functional testing, and production parts. [Learn More](https://jlc3dp.com/help/article/What-is-SLS-3D-Printing)
  - **WJP (White Jet Process):** Jets photopolymer resin and cures it with UV to achieve high precision and smooth surfaces. Supports multi-material and full-color printing for models and visual prototypes. [Learn More](https://jlc3dp.com/help/article/what-is-wjp-3d-printing)
  - **BJ (Binder Jetting):** Deposits binder onto metal powder and sinters it into finished parts. Offers efficient metal production with good surface quality after post-processing. [Learn More](https://jlc3dp.com/help/article/what-is-binder-jetting)

# Material
  ## Material Categories
  - **Resin:** Photopolymer materials cured by UV light, offering excellent surface quality and fine detail for high-precision prototypes and aesthetic parts. Used with SLA and WJP (full-color printing). [SLA](https://jlc3dp.com/help/catalog/87-Stereolithography-(SLA)) | [WJP](https://jlc3dp.com/help/catalog/2231-White-Jet-Process-(WJP))
  - **Nylon:** Powder materials processed by powder bed fusion, producing strong, tough, chemically resistant parts without support structures. Suited to functional parts and low-volume production. Used with MJF and SLS. [MJF](https://jlc3dp.com/help/catalog/88-Multi-Jet-Fusion-(MJF)) | [SLS](https://jlc3dp.com/help/catalog/91-Selective-Laser-Sintering-(SLS))
  - **Metal:** Powders such as stainless steel and titanium alloys, processed by melting or sintering, giving high density, strong mechanical performance, and heat resistance for industrial use. Used with SLM and BJ. [SLM](https://jlc3dp.com/help/catalog/89-Selective-Laser-Melting-(SLM)) | [BJ](https://jlc3dp.com/help/catalog/2701-Binder-Jetting-(BJ))
  - **Plastic:** Thermoplastic filaments processed by extrusion, offering durable, cost-effective parts in materials such as PLA, ASA, ABS, PA12-CF, and TPU. Used with FDM. [FDM](https://jlc3dp.com/help/catalog/90-Fused-Deposition-Modeling-(FDM))

# Surface Finish
  ## Descriptions
  - **Sanding:** Uses abrasive tools to remove support marks, layer lines, and surface imperfections for a smoother finish. Available as general sanding or fine sanding. Improves surface smoothness and consistency, and reduces visible layer lines.
  - **Sandblasting:** Uses high-pressure air to propel abrasive media onto the surface, improving texture and uniformity. Creates a consistent matte finish, removes layer lines and surface imperfections, and improves overall appearance.
  - **Spray Painting:** Applies a uniform paint coating for customized colors and enhanced appearance. Available colors are Sky Blue, Silver, White, Red, and Black. One color per part.
  - **Dyeing:** Uses dye penetration to color nylon parts with a uniform finish. Keeps the original surface texture without a coating. Color results may vary slightly between material batches and processing conditions.
  - **Polishing:** Uses mechanical polishing to improve surface smoothness and brightness. Only standard polishing is available. Reduces surface roughness and creates a refined metal finish. [Learn More](https://jlc3dp.com/help/article/metal-3d-printing-polishing-services)
  - **Oil Spraying:** Applies a clear coating for a glossy finish. Covers minor surface imperfections and enhances texture and appearance.

# Thread
