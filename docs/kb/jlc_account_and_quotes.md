# KB: the JLC account, its coupons, and reading its quotes

Audience: future LLM sessions. The user orders PCBs, assembled boards, 3D prints, CNC parts and fasteners from JLC (JLCPCB, JLC3DP, JLCCNC, JLCMC) from Canada; prices on the account are in CAD. Facts here were observed by the user or read
from their captures; the experiment-specific plan is in `spurious-force-balance` (`docs/fabrication.md`, `docs/kb/fab_ordering_notes.md`).

## What a session can and cannot see

- The quote pages (cart.jlcpcb.com/quote, jlc3dp.com, jlccnc.com) are scripts: a fetch returns only a file-upload box and "Calculated Price $0.00". The user's captures of the option lists are `docs/jlc/pcba_quote_ui.md` (Standard PCB/PCBA) and
  `docs/jlc/3dp_quote_ui.md`; the CNC sheet-metal and HDI captures were requested and not yet made (note: the captured page lists ENIG as the default surface finish; the user reports HASL with lead is).
- The `jlc` CLI (`npm @jlcpcb/cli` 0.5.0) searches the LCSC/EasyEDA parts catalogue (stock, price, basic/extended, Economic/Standard PCBA): it has no PCB, CNC or shipping quote. Use it for parts only.
- Therefore: never state a JLC price that is not in the user's words or captures, and ask for specific numbers, not for clicking tours.

## Coupons

`docs/jlc/coupons.md` is the ledger (expiry dates, what each is for, which are maxed out or irrelevant). Rules: each coupon is used once; the terms say one coupon per order but the user reports two can be used; coupons are applied at payment and in the cart,
not in the quote lines; a coupon cannot take a line below zero (put big coupons on dear lines). A coupon's expiry runs from the day the user clicks "Get Coupon" (reported 2026-11-04): a coupon that is not claimed yet has no date, and claiming early burns its term, so claim only when the order is about to be placed. Most new-user coupons end 2026-11-25 (CNC and PCB variants 11-26), others 2026-12-25. EasyEDA-design coupons need EasyEDA files. The "special offer" price of about
C$2.81 on the quote page is a new-user price for a 5-piece board up to 100 x 100 mm, not a coupon.

## Quote behaviour worth knowing

- The thickness field reverts to 1.6 mm at each upload; 1.6 mm made a 5-piece 208 x 60 mm order 0.34 kg and pushed shipping from C$2.10 to C$12.05. JLC's mass estimate is the bounding rectangle times thickness plus about 155 g of packing; the cheap tier's cliff is 0.3 kg.
- Fees seen on the 208 x 60 mm board: Engineering C$5.61, Board C$8.56, ENIG +C$25.25 (HASL C$0); quantity cannot go below 5.
- Standard PCBA needs boards of at least 70 x 70 mm with edge rails and a stencil per order; Economic is 10 x 10 mm, single side, $8 setup.
- JLCMC fasteners and the JLC3DP/JLCCNC material lists were not readable; ask the user for captures before planning with them.

## Reading the quote page from the JLCONE desktop app (2026-11-04, works only when the user launches it with a debug port)

`jlcone --remote-debugging-port=9222` (the user starts it; never restart or flag the app yourself) exposes Chromium's DevTools at 127.0.0.1:9222. The app keeps no quote or coupon data on disk (its profile `~/.config/jlcone` holds login state and cached scripts only; do not open the cookies). Read-only recipe, no clicks and no JLC API calls: `GET http://127.0.0.1:9222/json/list` lists the tabs (the quote page is `https://cart.jlcpcb.com/quote`, the account page `https://jlcpcb.com/user-center/`); connect to the tab's `webSocketDebuggerUrl` with `websocket.create_connection(url, suppress_origin=True)` (the browser rejects an Origin header with 403) and send `Runtime.evaluate` with `document.body.innerText` for the page text; input values (dimensions X/Y) come from `document.querySelectorAll('input')`; the selected option of a button group is the one whose computed border/background colour differs from its siblings (classes carry no "active"). The quote page shows after an upload: the detected size ("96.5x36mm" lists the long side first), the charge details (Special Offer, Calculated Price), a shipping estimate (default UPS line, far dearer than the postal tier shown at payment) and the weight. The selected option of a button group has a blue border (`rgb(0, 114, 252)`) and a pale blue fill: read that, not the heuristic of "the odd colour out", which misreads groups (it wrongly flagged ENIG on 2026-11-04). Clicks by `Input.dispatchMouseEvent` at the element's centre (after `scrollIntoView`) with 3-5 s between them work; each option change triggers a re-quote. The page may reset thickness (1.6 mm) after an upload: check it. Quote data read this way is in `docs/jlc/small_boards_panel_quote_2026-11-04.md`: the Panel fee is C$5.65 per extra design and a Single PCB gets the C$2.79 Special Offer. The coupon pages (`jlcpcb.com/coupon-center`, `jlcpcb.com/user-center/payments/coupon`) list every claimed coupon with amount, minimum and expiry date as plain text.

## Uploads and gotchas through the app (2026-11-04)

The user authorised uploading this repo's own Gerber zips for quotes (no order submitted, nothing saved to the cart; one order at a time, at most one per day, the user approves and pays). Upload: the quote page's `input[type=file]` exists only on the empty page and after clicking "Re-Upload"; set it with CDP `DOM.setFileInputFiles` (`DOM.getDocument`, `DOM.querySelectorAll` on `input[type=file]`, no native dialog; absolute path); wait 20-25 s, then read. Each upload resets the options (1.6 mm, OSP) and adds a "Deburring/Edge rounding" line on very small boards (default Yes, C$0.14-0.28). Choosing "Confirm Production file = Yes" opens a modal dialog that blocks the page until closed (its X cancels the choice) and costs C$1.46: close it before the next click. `Mechatronic Parts` and other product links that leave the quote site open outside the app. The wrapper `~/.local/bin/jlcone` (13% scale via `--force-device-scale-factor=1.13`) only takes effect when the shell resolves `jlcone` to it (`hash -r` or a new terminal; the 2026-11-04 launch used the cached `/usr/bin/jlcone`, so the scale did not apply).

## JLCMC inside the app (2026-11-05)

The account page's "Mechatronic Parts" link has `target=_blank`, so it opens in the system browser. What works: `Page.navigate` (CDP, on the account tab's `webSocketDebuggerUrl`) to `https://jlcmc.com/` keeps the site inside the app, and its pages read as text (`https://jlcmc.com/product/E06/washers` lists the 14 washer products; category URLs are `/product/<code>/<slug>`). A series page such as `/product/s/E06/EPDA/flat-washer-level-a` redirects to `/api/overseasFaPlatform/product/downloadPDF?serialCode=EPDA...` and shows the app's PDF viewer: `document.body.innerText` is empty and `Page.captureScreenshot` returned no result, so size tables and prices are not readable (do not fetch that API URL yourself). Navigating the tab back to `https://jlcpcb.com/user-center/` did not visibly change the tab title straight away: check the tab's URL, and tell the user if the account tab is left on JLCMC. The JLCMC news notice of 2026-11-02 ends combined ordering with JLCPCB, JLC3DP and JLCCNC: a JLCMC line is its own parcel with its own shipping. The account page's order list showed only the arm order when read, even though the user had placed `elec_board`; the page is a cached list, not a live one.
