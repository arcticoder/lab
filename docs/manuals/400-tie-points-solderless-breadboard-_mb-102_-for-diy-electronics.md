Circuit Connection:

TerminalStrips (Middle Section): Holes in the same row
(labeleda-e and f-j) are electrically connectedhorizontally.
The center divider allows for the insertion of DIP (Dual
In-line Package) ICs.
Distribution Bars (Side Rails): Marked with red (+) and blue
(-) lines, these holes are connected vertically and are
typically used for power supply and ground.
Insertion: Gently push the component leads into the holes. You
shouldfeel a slight resistance as the spring clip grabs the wire.
Testing: Once the circuit is built, apply power through the
distribution bars using a power module or jumper wires.
4. Maintenance & Storage

use compressed air to blow out the holes.
Storage: Store in a cool, dry place.Avoid heavy weights on topof
the boardto prevent warping.
